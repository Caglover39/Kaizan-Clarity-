  2. Who needs this app most? Describe the specific person — their role or situation, and the top 2–3 frustrations your app solves.
  3. What 3–4 features does your app absolutely need at launch? And roughly how many pages or screens do you picture?

Tech & API Integration Guide
App: TaskFlow — Freelance Project & Invoice Tracker

Your confirmed integrations: Stripe  ·  Supabase  ·  Resend

Stripe Setup

Purpose: collect $12/month or $99/year subscriptions.  When to add: after your core features work — never first.

Step 1 — Create your Stripe account. Go to stripe.com → sign up → complete business verification. Use Test Mode during development (toggle in the top-left of the Stripe dashboard).
Step 2 — Create your two products. In Stripe Dashboard → Products → Add Product: "TaskFlow Monthly" at $12/month and "TaskFlow Annual" at $99/year. Copy both Price IDs (starts with price_) — you'll need them in Lovable.
Step 3 — Add to Lovable. Go to Integrations → Stripe. Paste your Publishable Key (pk_test_... for development). Add the two Price IDs. Never use your Live key until launch.
⚠ Common mistake: using your Live API key during development. Always use Test keys (pk_test_...) until you’re ready to go live.
