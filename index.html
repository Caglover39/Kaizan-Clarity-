Prepare your answers in advance for a faster, more detailed session. The more specific you are, the better your outputs.

  1. What's your app idea? Tell me what it does in 2-3 sentences, and where the idea came from — a personal problem, something you noticed, or feedback you heard?
  2. Who needs this app most? Describe the specific person — their role or situation, and the top 2-3 frustrations your app solves for them.
  3. What 3-4 features does your app absolutely need at launch? And roughly how many pages or screens do you picture?
  4. Does your app have a name yet? And how should it look and feel — think colors, mood, and any apps whose design you love. Professional or playful? Minimal or bold? Light or dark?
  5. How will this app make money? Think about who pays, how much, and how often — subscription, one-time purchase, free with premium features, or something else?
  6. Are there 1-2 apps similar to yours, or that you admire the design of? And when are you hoping to launch — ASAP, within 30 days, 90 days, or no timeline yet?
  7. Answer yes, no, or not sure for each:
    • Do users need to create accounts and log in?
    • Will it collect payments from users?
    • Does it need to send emails or notifications?
    • Will users upload photos, videos, or files?
    • Does it need maps or location features?
    • Will it use AI to generate content, answer questions, or make recommendations?
    • Do you need an admin area to manage users or content?
  8. Is there anything the app should NOT do? Features you've seen in similar apps that frustrated you, or anything important to avoid?
  9. What's the moment when a user thinks "this is exactly what I needed"? Picture the first time someone successfully does the core thing your app was built for — what did they just see, do, or feel?
  10. Claude will recommend your tech stack based on your answers — you review and confirm. No technical knowledge required.
